# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Do not harass, threaten,
discriminate, disclose another person's private information, or post sexualized
or otherwise abusive material.

Keep discussions relevant and constructive. Explain technical disagreements
with reproducible evidence and accept reasonable maintainer decisions about
project scope. Do not pressure volunteers for personal information or immediate
responses.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Decisions should
be proportionate to the behavior and explained without exposing private reports.

For a conduct concern, request a private contact channel from the maintainer;
do not disclose sensitive details or personal information in a public thread.
A reporting channel must be established before inviting public contributions.
